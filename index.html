OUR LEGACY

A Tradition of Trust and Rigor

For over 50 years, P.A.O. Olorunnisola SAN & Co has stood as a beacon of legal professionalism, delivering exceptional results across Nigeria.

Established in 1969 in the historic city of Ilorin, Kwara State, the firm has consistently provided comprehensive legal services to individuals, corporate bodies, and government entities. Our practitioners are driven by zeal, promptness, and absolute integrity, ensuring that no stone is left unturned in safeguarding our clients' interests.

Whether navigating complex maritime disputes, securing corporate intellectual property, recovering substantial debt, or representing clients in sensitive election petitions, we bring seasoned knowledge and relentless determination to every brief.

OUR ANCHOR

Chief P.A.O. Olorunnisola, SAN
